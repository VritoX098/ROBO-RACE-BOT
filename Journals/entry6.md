Connected the ESP32 to the L298N today. This is where things got messy.

The initial wiring was a complete disaster cause I used too many jumper wires and it looked like spaghetti. I couldn't tell which wire was going where and ended up crossing a few connections. The motors wouldn't spin and I was lowkey panicking.

Spent like an hour just tracing wires and checking connections with the multimeter. Realized I swapped IN1 and IN2 on one side. Fixed that and the motors started spinning! But only in one direction?? Turns out I had soldered the wires of the motor wrong, now I have to resolder it.
 ![image](https://cdn.hackclub.com/019fabba-cc88-7792-b6f3-eebfe54d42f8/image.png)

After all that I organized the wiring and used some black tape to make it look less chaotic. Still messy but better.
