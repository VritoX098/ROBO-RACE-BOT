I finally decided to build a 4WD ESP32 robot. Watched way too many tutorials today. My YouTube history is basically all "ESP32 robot tutorial" and "L298N motor driver explained". I'm a total beginner to hardware so most of it went over my head but I think I got the gist.

Components I'm using: ESP32 dev board, L298N motor driver, 4 BO motors with wheels, 3x 18650 batteries in a 3S holder. Also lots of jumper wires cause I know I'll mess up the connections.

gathered all the components, now its time to make chassis.

Although I have made 3d model of the chassis, I am going to cut ply wood because I don't have access to 3d printers. Cutting the ply was not an easy task. I broke few before finalizing the actual design.

![image](https://cdn.hackclub.com/019fa182-e507-7a7d-8fa5-63be198f5ba3/2026-07-27_08-25-15.png) 



Then I mounted the BO motors onto the chassis using hot glue gun.
The motors were brand new so I had to solder wires in them.
This took time because if I soldering the wrong size of the motor then 2 of my motor will rotates in one direction and other 2 in another direction so I had to be a bit careful there.

![image](https://cdn.hackclub.com/019fa16c-214f-7273-a3f4-f0e6db36cb70/image.png)

Attached the battery holder and it was now giving the vibe of actual robo car.
